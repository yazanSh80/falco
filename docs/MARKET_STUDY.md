## 1. Executive Summary

FalcoElectrec addresses a significant market gap in the Palestinian home services sector. There is currently no dominant digital platform connecting homeowners with qualified electrical and home service professionals. This market study examines the opportunity, competitive landscape, target demographics, and business model viability.

### Key Findings:

- **Market Size**: ~1.2 million households in Palestine require periodic home services
- **Digital Adoption**: Smartphone penetration at 78%+ creates strong mobile platform potential
- **Competition**: No established local competitor; global platforms don't serve the region
- **Revenue Model**: Commission-based (5% on transactions >700 NIS) ensures sustainable growth

## 3. Target Market Segments

### 3.1 Customer Segments

#### Primary: Urban Homeowners (West Bank Cities)

- **Demographics**: Ages 25-55, middle to upper income
- **Locations**: Ramallah, Hebron, Nablus, Bethlehem
- **Pain Points**: 
  - Difficulty finding reliable technicians
  - No price transparency
  - Quality assurance concerns
  - Scheduling difficulties

#### Secondary: Property Managers & Landlords

- **Characteristics**: Multiple properties, regular maintenance needs
- **Value Proposition**: Bulk service management, trusted provider network

#### Tertiary: Small Business Owners

- **Characteristics**: Commercial properties, office maintenance
- **Value Proposition**: Quick response, professional service

### 3.2 Engineer/Technician Segments

#### Primary: Independent Electricians

- **Profile**: 5-15 years experience, looking for steady work
- **Pain Points**:
  - Inconsistent customer flow
  - Marketing/advertising costs
  - Payment collection issues

#### Secondary: Multi-skilled Technicians

- **Profile**: Electrical + plumbing + A/C skills
- **Value Proposition**: More job opportunities, portfolio showcase

#### Tertiary: Service Companies

- **Profile**: Small teams of 3-10 technicians
- **Value Proposition**: Job distribution, brand building

## 5. Business Model

### 5.1 Revenue Streams

#### Primary: Transaction Commission
- **Standard Rate**: 5% on completed jobs >700 NIS
- **Free Tier**: No commission on jobs ≤700 NIS (market penetration)
- **Rationale**: Encourages platform adoption, captures value on larger jobs

#### Secondary: Engineer Subscriptions (Future)
- **Monthly Plan**: Enhanced visibility, priority listings
- **Estimated Price**: 50-100 NIS/month

#### Tertiary: Premium Services (Future)
- Instant matching (rush fee)
- Featured engineer listings
- Business accounts

### 5.2 Commission Structure Analysis

| Job Value (NIS) | Commission Rate | Commission (NIS) |

## 10. Conclusion

FalcoElectrec is positioned to capture a nascent but growing market for digital home services in Palestine. With no established competitors, strong digital infrastructure, and a proven business model from global analogues, the platform has significant potential for success.

### Success Factors:

1. ✅ First-mover advantage in Palestinian market
2. ✅ Low customer acquisition cost (social/referral)
3. ✅ Scalable technology platform
4. ✅ Clear value proposition for both sides
5. ✅ Sustainable commission model

2. Recruit initial engineer cohort (25 professionals)
